    How to farm it

      Farming is not a guarantee of a token. A protocol may never launch one, and a farm plan is a record of activity, not a promise of a reward. Never pay to claim, and never enter a seed phrase anywhere.

      Live tokens, for comparison

      Protocols that already trade, by market cap. They are not ranked with the board — the market has already priced them. Use them to sense-check the model's multiples: a live token's market cap against its TVL is the multiple the market actually paid.

      How the model works

      Implied value follows only from the snapshot's own figures. A launch is priced by what the business is, not only by how big it is:

      implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × chains

      Category multiple
      Fee-capturing curators and allocators earn the most per dollar of TVL (0.38), then lending and real-world-asset protocols (0.32), then exchanges and yield (0.26), then bridges and staking wrappers (0.16–0.18), whose value is mostly the underlying asset, not a new token. The full table is in the data file.
      Growth
      1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%) × 0.6. A protocol growing fast is worth more than one the same size that is shrinking.
      Maturity
      0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4. Older protocols are steadier; a protocol with no listing date is scored as 365 days old.
      Chains
      1 + min(chain count, 8) × 0.025. A protocol on more chains has wider distribution, so a launch reaches more users.
      Early rows
      The watchlist is the floor, never the ceiling. Pre-token protocols below the $50M floor are added by TVL, up to 160 rows, because the radar exists to find the early ones.
      Excluded
      Centralised exchanges, versioned pools (a name ending in a release), and any protocol that already has a token are not ranked. Wrapped and staked products are not ranked as launches of their own.
